Hello, I’m Muhammad Haroon Banatwalla, but you can just call me Haroon. I am a data analyst with more than 4 years of experience working with Healthcare data at Epic Systems. I am currently searching for data analyst or business intelligence analyst roles in any domain. 

Some of my projects include: 
- [Healthcare Claims Analysis](https://github.com/banatwallam5/healthcare-claims-analysis-SQL-): I used synthetic patient data created using Synthea. Here I modeled the data as a star schema in MySQL and used that to answer several questions that an organization may care about. One of the findings was that that the costs per visit were the same for organizations with higher patient volume as they were for lower patient volumes proving that patient volume has little impact on costs. 

- [CO2 Emissions Project- Python](https://github.com/banatwallam5/Python-CO2-emission-project-with-clean-up-and-analysis): Here I used the [OWID's CO2 data ](https://github.com/owid/co2-data) and used python to first clean up data, then reduced the countries looked at to 96 which accounted for approximately 95% of the data. I then set a baseline of 2015 and used that to calculate and rank countries by the most positive and negative change in CO2 emission per capita. I then picked the top three countries in either category and researched the events in said countries that may help explain the trend seen in the data and used that to inform how countries are doing in their efforts to reduce CO2 emissions. We found that of the 6 countries looked at only the Netherlands' change was a conscious effort intended to reduce CO2 emissions. As for the rest, the change was caused by outside factors such as civil unrest or oil-price collapse. 

- [Ecommerce- Power BI Project](https://github.com/banatwallam5/POWER-BI-project): Here I used ecommerce data built on the star schema to create visualizations and answer questions in Power BI. I used DAX and Power Query to help modify the data and create parameters and calculated fields as required. Along this project I caught and fixed a modeling bug caused by Power BI's auto Date/Time setting which was silently creating duplicate hidden calendar tables.
